# Nextendo Network

**Bringing online multiplayer back to the Nintendo Switch.**

Community-run replacement servers — accounts, friends, matchmaking and peer-to-peer play for your favourite Switch games.

[🌐 nextendo.network](https://nextendo.network) · [💬 Discord](https://discord.gg/XPfeCMwnzQ)

</div>

---

## About

Nextendo Network provides private online servers for Nintendo Switch games, so you can keep playing online with friends. It reimplements the account system, matchmaking and networking layers, and plays through our **Ryujinx-Nextendo** emulator build — see the repositories below.

## Supported games

🏎️ **Mario Kart 8 Deluxe** · 🦑 **Splatoon 2** · 👊 **Super Smash Bros. Ultimate** — and more in progress.

## Get started

1. Create your account at **[nextendo.network](https://nextendo.network)**
2. Download the latest **Ryujinx-Nextendo** build (see the pinned repository)
3. Sign in and play — full setup guides and support are on our **[Discord](https://discord.gg/XPfeCMwnzQ)**
