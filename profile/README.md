# Nextendo Network

**Bringing online multiplayer back to the Nintendo Switch.**

Community-run replacement servers: accounts, friends, matchmaking and peer-to-peer play for your favorite Switch games.

[🌐 nextendo.network](https://nextendo.network) · [💬 Discord](https://discord.gg/XPfeCMwnzQ)

</div>

---

## About

Nextendo Network is a community-run replacement for the official online service. It reimplements accounts, matchmaking and networking so supported games can be played online again, with no manual configuration, custom hosts file, or SSL bypass needed on your end.

It is an independent, non-commercial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Nintendo. It ships no Nintendo code, keys or copyrighted assets: you must provide your own legally dumped games and system files.

## Ways to play

- **PC with Ryujinx-Nextendo**: the Ryujinx fork with online built in. Windows, Linux, macOS. See the [Ryujinx-Nextendo](https://github.com/NextendoNetwork/Ryujinx-Nextendo) repository.
- **PC with Citron-Nextendo**: the Citron fork with online built in. Windows, Linux. See the [citron-nextendo](https://github.com/NextendoNetwork/citron-nextendo) repository.
- **Real Switch with Prelude**: homebrew that connects an Atmosphere console to Nextendo Network, reversibly, no Nintendo account needed. See the [Prelude-Nro](https://github.com/NextendoNetwork/Prelude-Nro) repository.

## Supported games

Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, Splatoon 2, Splatoon 3, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, Animal Crossing: New Horizons, Luigi's Mansion 3, Super Mario Maker 2, Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury, Super Mario Bros. Wonder, Super Mario Odyssey, Mario Party Superstars, Mario Tennis Aces, Mario Golf: Super Rush, Mario Strikers: Battle League (work in progress), Super Mario Bros. 35, Kirby's Dream Buffet, Clubhouse Games: 51 Worldwide Classics, Minecraft: Nintendo Switch Edition, Monster Hunter Generations Ultimate, ARMS, Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker, Diablo III, Crash Team Racing Nitro-Fueled. More in progress; see the pinned repositories and the [wiki](https://wiki.nextendo.network) for details on each.

## Companion apps

Nextendo App for [Android](https://github.com/NextendoNetwork/Nextendo-App-Android) and [iOS](https://github.com/NextendoNetwork/Nextendo-App-Ios): your account, friends, play sessions and cloud saves in your pocket. The app never sees your password, sign-in goes through the website.

## Get started

1. Create your account at [nextendo.network](https://nextendo.network)
2. Pick a client above and follow its README
3. Sign in and play. Full setup guides and support are on [Discord](https://discord.gg/XPfeCMwnzQ)
